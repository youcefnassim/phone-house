## Financial controls and outstanding risk

- Exact decimal strings and pre-cast scale checks are preserved; no rounding, expense limits, negative cash treatment, discrepancy adjustments, credit eligibility or statutory classifications invented.
- Journals remain technical reconciliation buckets, not approved accounting. Reversal insertion now explicitly rejects until a safe mirrored-ledger/cash/stock policy is approved; immutable journal/audit evidence cannot be edited to simulate a reversal.
- Purchase finance NULL or absent means unknown. No cost is accepted on operational receiving and no cost appears on operational DTOs. Owner reads independently require verified user-bound authorization in SQL; field-safe source is not proven effective RLS.
- Model and unit barcode uniqueness remain separate namespaces; unified scanner namespace acceptance is still a release consideration. No hardware acceptance claimed.
- Broad service-role privileges and privileged operators are not security substitutes; ordinary reads/commands must retain user-bound credentials. Safe table grants exist for all new public tables, with explicit RLS/function ACLs.
- Open-shift sales/repayment cash inference is reduced by controlled projections, but no source change establishes complete blind-close confidentiality against cached UI/session or privileged external paths; hosted role/field tests remain mandatory.

**Release stop:** all database findings remain uncertified until independent authorized database and hosted acceptance; policy-dependent execution remains disabled even after test approval.
