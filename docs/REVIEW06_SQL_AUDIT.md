## Strong existing structural properties

Basic foreign keys, separate phone and accessory tables, RLS enabled on every table, one drawer per store UNIQUE, one OPEN shift per drawer partial UNIQUE, M01 facility CHECK, decimal storage rather than FLOAT, and the auth.uid-gated owner finance function are useful starting structures. They do not establish atomic inventory/finance correctness. Do not assume one shift per day.

## Unapproved policy boundary

Original OD-01–OD-18 remains byte-preserved. No guessed tax, discount threshold, warranty period, credit eligibility, expense taxonomy, reversal treatment, transfer partial receipt or offline auth duration is added. OD-11 blocks unsupported partial receipt; OD-08 refunds require eligibility; OD-12 credit needs explicit arrangement; OD-13 bank-confirmation roles need approval; OD-14 accounting policy incomplete; OD-17 adjustments unapproved; OD-18 offline identity/conflict policy incomplete. The facility 1200-month limit is an existing technical display bound, not owner policy. Equal-installment storage is insufficient for general contract-specific schedules and must be redesigned without rounding.
